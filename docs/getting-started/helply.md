# Ask Helply

Helply is an AI assistant in the Schuly web app. It can answer questions about your school data,
such as "What exams do I have this week?" You need a Schuly account.

Helply isn't available on every server. If it's turned off, the server hasn't set it up.

::: warning Your data goes to an AI service
To answer you, Helply sends your messages, your attachments, and the data it reads to the AI service
your server uses. AI answers can be wrong, so check anything important.
:::

## Start a chat

1. Open [app.schuly.dev](https://app.schuly.dev) and sign in.
2. In the sidebar, under **AI**, choose **Helply**.
3. Type a question and press Enter. To start a new line, press Shift+Enter.

To attach files, drag them into the chat. You can attach up to 5 files per message, each up to 20 MB.

## Choose what Helply can use

Open **Helply settings** to set what Helply can read or change for each area, such as grades or mail.
Your changes apply to your next message.

## Approve changes

Helply asks before it does anything that changes your data, such as deleting a mail. Choose
**Approve** or **Deny**.

## Run code on your files

If your server offers it, turn on **Workspace (run code)** in **Helply settings**. Helply can then
run code on your attachments, for example to make a chart of your grades. The workspace has no
internet access.

## How long chats are kept

Schuly deletes conversations automatically after 30 days, along with their attachments. You can
also delete them yourself at any time.
